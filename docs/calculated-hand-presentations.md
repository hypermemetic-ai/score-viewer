# Calculated hand presentations

The calculator chooses performing hands jointly with fingers and physical contacts. Printed staves and independently owned source voices stay musical source facts. A new calculation uses `calculate` by default; explicitly requesting `retain-imported` retains that division as editorial inference, without creating composer authority.

New requests remove only the exact historical Viewer-generated hand-only `editorial-hand-retention` record after checking its owner/source guard and installed hand. Genuine explicit source requirements and guarded current owner hand/finger/contact choices remain constraints. Unknown provenance, stale guards and earlier blanket history locks require review and return a conflict. Personal history and undo records do not freeze unrelated owners.

A retained-edition read is separate from a new calculation. `read-retained` admits its complete saved layer/pages under their existing contract without claiming that it was recalculated under a newer policy. Explicit Generate requests use the current policy. New readiness reuse requires the matching versioned preparation identity: immutable source, canonical complete assignments, physical keyboard/profile, context, policy/authority, search budget, recipe/engine and actual payload. Ordering owner assignments does not change identity. This metadata proves compatibility, not painted source coverage or successful continuous movement.

Changed hands use the reviewed ISO `score-presentation` factory and complete source-owned preparation. The cold reader independently reconstructs the expected identity from original source bytes, the complete saved finger/contact assignment, admitted authority and context; it strongly validates provenance and rerenders the saved hand map without an optimizer. Actual score, source inventory/paint coverage, raw baseline, seated pages, literal seat bytes and owned carrier geometry must match. A saved receipt or outer hash cannot substitute for that replay. Already derived editions with changed fingers or context use fresh preparation even when every hand stays the same.

New request identities use `viewer-piano-preparation-v3`, binding the calculated-engine contract and the exact annotation-ink policy. Frozen V1/V2 identities retain their original hash meanings for cold reading; an earlier identity cannot authorize a new-policy edition. New calculated editions carry `viewer-calculated-hand-edition-v2` receipts; historical receiptV1 drawing remains unchanged. Recipe baseline hashes literal raw SVG before inert seat attributes, while output hashes literal seated SVG. Both use the same literal `JSON.stringify(seats)` bytes. Canonical assignment/context identity hashes are distinct conventions.

The reader reserves all 1–5 annotation outlines beyond fresh owned head, mask and complete duration bounds. It independently inventories the entire actual SVG, including foreign voices, rests, curves and text, and admits annotations against that ink and one another. Placement tries the original column first, then the smallest cleared horizontal displacement within half the owning glyph em. The entire stack stays inside independently derived neighboring original source-column midpoints and above the complete RH carrier or below the complete LH carrier. Shared stacks and written continuations retain their affiliation.

General curves reserve their control hulls. Only a recognized monotone, noncrossing paired cubic closed ribbon receives a bounded subdivided cover of both boundaries, filled interior and endpoint connectors, including affine/stroke padding and outward rounding. Stroked primitives retain conservative cap/miter extents. Under the exact Viewer footer policy, positively matched footer text uses the verified licensed face and complete glyph-cell bounds with identical supported shaping, end-anchor and transform rules in drawing and admission; unknown text/features retain conservative bands or refuse. Font/metric/load/family failure retains the prior edition before staging. Literal ISO page, seat, geometry and recipe evidence remain unchanged. Unsupported SVG paint/positioning or a failed bounded fit refuses the new edition. Legacy annotation behavior stays under its existing contract. All-five replacement is optional; actual replacement failure leaves admitted annotations and source-linked correction seats available.

Unsupported geometry, source coverage, grace choice or recipe retains the exact prior archive. The calculator currently refuses unrepresented grace actions before producing a layer; ISO independently guards required grace-hand choices. No fixed-page stem flipping is permitted.

Contexts belong to each presentation. An omitted descriptor context preserves historical inheritance from the collection root; explicit null uses the immutable source's own reference. New assembly snapshots companions' effective clocks before changing the primary, and independently validates each descriptor and root/primary affiliation. A new request stages a changed context in detached calculation variables until complete fresh assembly; it never re-pins an existing layer to another clock. A reference already declared in immutable source cannot be overridden. Retained reads must match their saved clock, including at the reader worker boundary.

That archival seam does not classify development editorial namespaces as new operator authority, alter original bytes or loosen fresh calculated-hand admission.

Prior packages and personal journals remain associated with their edition. Only actively guarded compatible corrections can carry into a changed edition. Old-hand layers, undo and practice history stay with their own pages rather than appearing in a new edition's finger-only history. Installation stages immutable package/state versions before one library-pointer update; failed writes, cancellation and supersession retain current pointers.

A declared personal-journal pointer must resolve to its saved state. Normal reading, earlier-edition restoration and pending saves refuse a missing declared journal before writing; another bare/legacy journal or an empty state cannot substitute for it. Bare/legacy fallback is available only where no explicit pointer exists, including a legitimate first open with no saved state.

New prepared editions append their semantic presentation revision to state/practice/performance keys and personal binding. Legacy keys remain unchanged. Each retained package also keeps its personal-file pointers, so later calculations cannot replace its original journal even when the physical hand map is compatible. New prepared editions do not silently read legacy sidecars; explicitly guarded compatible choices are carried during preparation.
