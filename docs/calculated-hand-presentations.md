# Calculated hand presentations

The calculator chooses performing hands jointly with fingers and physical contacts. Printed staves and independently owned source voices stay musical source facts. A new calculation uses `calculate` by default; explicitly requesting `retain-imported` retains that division as editorial inference, without creating composer authority.

New requests remove only the exact historical Viewer-generated hand-only `editorial-hand-retention` record after checking its owner/source guard and installed hand. Genuine explicit source requirements and guarded current owner hand/finger/contact choices remain constraints. Unknown provenance, stale guards and earlier blanket history locks require review and return a conflict. Personal history and undo records do not freeze unrelated owners.

A retained-edition read is separate from a new calculation. `read-retained` admits its complete saved layer/pages under their existing contract without claiming that it was recalculated under a newer policy. Explicit Generate requests use the current policy. New readiness reuse requires the matching versioned preparation identity: immutable source, canonical complete assignments, physical keyboard/profile, context, policy/authority, search budget, recipe/engine and actual payload. Ordering owner assignments does not change identity. This metadata proves compatibility, not painted source coverage or successful continuous movement.

Changed hands still require the reviewed compatible ISO complete engraving contract. Preparation must produce matching pages/seats and independently reimport them with strong source/authority and painted ownership admission before a successful result. Unsupported geometry, source coverage, grace choice or recipe retains the exact prior archive. No fixed-page stem flipping is permitted. All-five replacement remains separate from complete annotation readiness.

Prior packages and personal journals remain associated with their edition. Only actively guarded compatible corrections can carry into a changed edition. Old-hand layers, undo and practice history stay with their own pages rather than appearing in a new edition's finger-only history. Installation stages immutable package/state versions before one library-pointer update; failed writes, cancellation and supersession retain current pointers.

A declared personal-journal pointer must resolve to its saved state. Normal reading, earlier-edition restoration and pending saves refuse a missing declared journal before writing; another bare/legacy journal or an empty state cannot substitute for it. Bare/legacy fallback is available only where no explicit pointer exists, including a legitimate first open with no saved state.

New prepared editions append their semantic presentation revision to state/practice/performance keys and personal binding. Legacy keys remain unchanged. Each retained package also keeps its personal-file pointers, so later calculations cannot replace its original journal even when the physical hand map is compatible. New prepared editions do not silently read legacy sidecars; explicitly guarded compatible choices are carried during preparation.

The implementation and exact reviewed ISO dependency are coordinated separately from native delivery. Frontend checks do not establish installed app, physical MIDI/audio/pedal or player acceptance. The existing native release-carrier scripts and paired-delivery specification govern any later coordinated delivery; migration history is not evidence of the current carrier's availability. Paid Zelda source remains private and its proposed split is a comparison hypothesis.
