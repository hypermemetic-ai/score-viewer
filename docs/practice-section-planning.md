# Musical practice section planning

`src/practice-sizing.ts` and `src/practice-boundaries.ts` provide pure source analysis for the bounded-exercise catalog. They do not change playback, saved attainment, or musical source files. The initial `onset-musical-boundaries-v1` policy uses five distinct intended attack moments per estimated practice second, a five-second target (25 onsets), and a preferred four-to-six-second span (20–30 onsets). Simultaneous chords and hands count once; sounding note owners count at their genuine starts, while written tie continuations add no onset. Assessment still retains every pitch and hold.

Five is a calibrated backend parameter, not an experimentally established memory capacity. Reference tempo cancels from this fully normalized sizing clock; actual transport retains its existing score reference and player-selected speed. Half-section cue and continuation targets are each 2.5 estimated seconds, or 12.5 onsets. This does not promise 2.5 actual seconds at the player's selected tempo.

The planner indexes exact transposition-invariant pitch/timing windows of at least 12 and at most 40 onsets, spanning at most eight written bars. Chord pitches and stream relationships remain in those keys. Recurrence confidence uses independent nonoverlapping copies, caps its frequency contribution, and uses the strongest window rather than adding up overlapping matches. A window whose preceding bar also repeats is discounted to one tenth of its support: it is probably a continuing trunk, not a newly established beginning. Shared-prefix ends receive no automatic phrase-boundary reward.

Authored repeat, double and meter boundaries supply stronger support. Actual global silent gaps, long onset spacing, source phrase ends and sustained register/pitch-content/texture change supply weaker support. A rest in one voice is not a global rest while another voice sounds; a spacer is not rest evidence. Texture change is a supporting proxy and is never labeled a cadence or a full harmonic analysis.

All boundaries are selected together by deterministic dynamic programming. Each span pays a 0.5 section cost, four times squared relative target deviation, twelve times squared deviation outside the preferred span, and a separate penalty for fewer than ten onsets. A cut through a genuine held note, short connected source phrase or supplied technique obligation costs five. Boundary support reduces the cost by 0.85 times support, capped at three. These constants and supporting weights are frozen in the policy fingerprint; changing the policy requires an explicit catalog/evidence version transition.

Normal bars remain whole. Bars containing more than 30 onset moments admit cuts at real onset ticks, preserving simultaneous attacks. Intra-measure cuts retain their exact start/end ticks alongside the containing bar indices; callers must not replace them with whole-bar ranges. A cut through an unavoidable hold records that obligation for source-aware preparation and rendering rather than pretending it disappeared.

Bounded inspection on the retained No. 14 fixture yields sixteen four-bar originals: fifteen have 24 onset moments and the final one has 19. Constructed controls produce two-bar sections at twelve onsets per bar, an eight-bar section at three per bar, a supported local four/three/four-bar partition, and two internal sections for a 51-onset single bar. A nine-bar six-onset-per-bar source produces 24- and 30-onset sections, avoiding a six-onset leftover. These are diagnostics, not human musical acceptance or proof of universal optimality.

`planPracticeSections(source, {protectedRelations})` returns the frozen policy/fingerprint, distinct onset positions, bounded boundary evidence and original source sections. Boundary metadata covers every intended onset position, including excerpt-only interior positions; `sectionCandidate` distinguishes the cuts available to ordinary section planning. Thus cue/continuation snapping cannot accidentally treat an unprofiled interior cut through a hold as safer than a bar boundary through the same hold. Manual adapters can supply genuine technique intervals. `choosePracticeExcerptBoundary(profile, bounds)` reuses the same profile for cue/continuation snapping; the caller must first restrict those bounds to the genuine shared trunk or current divergence episode.

Input is limited to 2,000 written bars and 100,000 note owners. Indexed recurrence windows, sweep-based sounding obligations and a bounded partition lookback avoid a resident all-note similarity matrix. The path search stops when a span exceeds 60 onsets, with a 4,000-cut guard so sparse silent measures do not prematurely truncate an otherwise valid workload span. Sparse sustained passages can have long actual duration despite small onset workload. Analysis does not infer full tonal form, learned player familiarity, or fingering complexity. Weak structural support therefore leaves the onset target in control, while the player retains surgical exercise exclusion.

Resource-contained diagnostics processed a 2,000-bar/24,000-onset source into 1,000 sections with 2,001 section candidates and 24,001 profiled positions, and a deliberately oversized 100,000-onset single measure into 3,704 sections with 100,001 candidates. Observed analysis times were about 0.22 and 0.79 seconds on the development host before integration; these are bounded local observations, not a UI latency guarantee.
