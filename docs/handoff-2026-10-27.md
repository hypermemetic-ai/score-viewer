## How we work from here (agreed 2026-10-27)

- **Mechanical work** iterates freely and merges on green gates: crashes, refusals, speed, clear regressions.
- **Taste work** follows these steps: practice feel, layout, controls and wording.
  1. understand the intent;
  2. gather the cases;
  3. the owner decides in one sitting;
  4. implement;
  5. the owner tries it in the installed app.
- Engraving changes arrive from the notation thread as finished batches. The viewer repins ISO at points we choose, never commit by commit, and every repin is a delivery: rehearsal, seal, native build, install, and the Mac zip as a download.

## Agreed designs not built yet

1. **Scripted rehearsal** (agreed 2026-10-23). Today the rehearsal harness lives outside the repository, and every delivery re-scripts it. The agreed shape:
   - a driver that alone knows screens and keys, built on the app's own key bindings;
   - the app declares its state through stable `data-*` attributes;
   - the score × layout × section inventory is discovered from the library;
   - invariants instead of golden files: every piano score can be practised, no error banner, an early press runs exactly once, pages are present, non-piano scores offer no practice;
   - changed pages and timings are reports, not gates.

   No CI: `npm run rehearse`, plus an AGENTS.md gate line. It runs on studio scores, and on the owner's data from a local path only. Build this before the navigation overhaul.
2. **Navigation overhaul** (agreed 2026-10-22). Check it against the current app before starting, because the P menu (#159) landed earlier.
   - The library opens a **score menu**: Practice (P, preselected) and Score (V). Esc goes up one level everywhere.
   - Practice opens the sections, then the steps **Read · Fingers · Speed**, with the first unfinished step preselected. The order is a nudge, never a gate.
   - **Read** works through position pairs with both hands, after an on-ramp: the first position alone, in the hand that plays first. It loops, and counts as done after one full loop.
   - **Fingers** is done when every note of the section has a finger. **Speed** is done at the target speed.
   - **Memory** (connections and contrasts) unlocks when two adjacent sections are done; it is not designed yet.
   - Every piano score must be practicable. Non-piano scores show Practice greyed out, never as cross-instrument practice.

## Known findings

- **A press lost at paint:** E, F or P pressed while the "Opening" dialog closes can be lost (found 2026-10-23). The 10-27 rehearsal saw P ignored 3 times in about 140 presses on the new build, 1 in 100 on the previous main, and 0 in 20 on the 10-23 laptop build; a second press works. The scripted rehearsal's "an early press runs exactly once" invariant should cover it.
