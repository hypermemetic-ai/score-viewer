### Problems found

All of these predate this delivery:
- **The reader gets stuck after a refused hand choice.** On No. 13, L on the RH pickup is refused, and then "Choices haven't been saved" blocks leaving the score. It was not triggered on the target machine.
- **Jankó-only score file imports fail on a fresh Conventional profile.**
  - This affected early-format scores, which can't gain a conventional view.
  - PR #135 imported such a score on its own Jankó keyboard instead. That fallback was the wrong idea: the two layouts read the same notation, so every score must open on either layout.
  - Superseded in source: such a score now imports, opens and switches on either layout, showing its own existing pages on the conventional layout with nothing engraved (see [engraving saved editions again](saved-edition-reengraving.md#either-keyboard-layout)).
  - Neither change is installed or observed in the native viewer yet.
- **The installed No. 13 has no fingerings of its own.** The edited fingerings are in other scores.
- **Remote input is uneven.** Windows-MCP `Shortcut` keys (Esc) do not reach the WebView. `Type` keys do reach it (O, R), but E did not enter edit mode. The toolbar appears only on a bottom-edge hover.
