# practice-exercises Specification

## Purpose

Provide manageable musical learning units, bounded adjacent connections and focused contrast practice while retaining genuine source identities and earned evidence.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Distinct onset workload sizing
Section sizing SHALL count each distinct intended score attack time once across simultaneous notes and performing parts. Its initial sizing rate SHALL be five onsets per estimated practice second, with a five-second target and preferred four-to-six-second span. This sizing clock SHALL NOT change actual playback tempo or assessment obligations.

#### Scenario: Chords and ties
- **WHEN** six successive attack times include chords, simultaneous hands and held tie continuations
- **THEN** sizing counts six onsets regardless of the number of simultaneous pitches
- **AND** assessment retains all required pitches and durations, while tie continuations add no new onset

#### Scenario: No 14 calibration
- **WHEN** an ordinary four-measure passage contains 24 distinct onsets
- **THEN** its estimated sizing duration is 4.8 seconds and it lies within the preferred range
- **AND** an ordinary eight-measure 48-onset span does not qualify merely because it lasts roughly five reference-performance seconds

### Requirement: Evidence-sensitive musical section boundaries
Original sections SHALL cover the written source in order with locally variable lengths. Boundary choices SHALL combine substantial repeated pitch/timing patterns including transposition, actual gaps/long notes, authored boundaries and sustained texture change. Strong evidence SHALL permit greater movement from the workload target; weak evidence SHALL favor nearby cuts. Meter alone SHALL NOT force one global section size.

#### Scenario: Three-measure exception
- **WHEN** a strongly supported three-measure unit occurs among four-measure units
- **THEN** the planner can retain that unit and start following sections at its actual end
- **AND** it does not average the exception into a different piece-wide measure count

#### Scenario: Uniform rhythm or ambiguous structure
- **WHEN** consecutive notes have identical durations
- **THEN** pitch-pattern repetition remains available as structural evidence
- **WHEN** structural evidence is weak
- **THEN** cuts remain close to the workload target at safe source boundaries without inventing harmonic certainty

#### Scenario: Source safety and leftovers
- **WHEN** a candidate lies within a simultaneous attack, held obligation or short connected source gesture
- **THEN** the planner preserves its musical obligations and prefers a safer nearby cut
- **AND** consecutive boundaries are chosen together to avoid an unnecessary tiny final section
- **AND** structural skip records do not masquerade as audible rests

### Requirement: Independent bounded adjacent connections
The exercise catalog SHALL contain every original section and each independent pair of consecutive originals. Adjacent pairs SHALL overlap without becoming a recursive tree. Learning connections SHALL NOT produce larger joined exercises. Catalog membership SHALL exist before completion-driven unlocking.

#### Scenario: Three learned originals
- **WHEN** A, B and C are original sections
- **THEN** A–B and B–C are distinct catalog exercises with independent attainment
- **AND** no A–B–C exercise is generated by their completion
- **AND** unlocking either pair does not expand the Total denominator

### Requirement: Transposition-aware section similarity
Contrast candidates SHALL compare original sections using matching note events at relative onset and duration under one constant pitch transposition. Similarity SHALL be matching-note count divided by the larger section note count. Pairs at least 75% but less than 100% SHALL qualify, regardless of distance in the piece. Similarity SHALL NOT grant completion credit.

#### Scenario: Threshold and exact transposition
- **WHEN** 18 of 24 note events match under one constant transposition
- **THEN** the pair meets the 75% threshold
- **WHEN** all note events match under that transposition
- **THEN** no contrast exercise is created
- **AND** a six-of-24 match does not qualify

#### Scenario: Chord workload and similarity differ
- **WHEN** a section contains simultaneous pitches
- **THEN** similarity accounts for its individual note events without double matching an event
- **AND** section workload still counts each attack moment only once

### Requirement: Focused contrast excerpts
Each contrast exercise SHALL alternate source-faithful A and B excerpts around a divergence. Each excerpt SHALL target a 2.5 estimated-second shared lead-in and a 2.5-second divergent continuation, snapped using musical boundary evidence. A shorter available trunk SHALL shorten the cue. Sustained reconvergence SHALL end the continuation early; an isolated matching event SHALL NOT end it.

#### Scenario: Short trunk and divergent endings
- **WHEN** the available shared trunk is shorter than 12–13 onsets
- **THEN** the excerpt uses that shorter trunk without importing a previous divergent episode
- **AND** it includes the divergence and an approximately 12–13-onset continuation when available
- **AND** A and B retain their actual source pitches and source locations rather than being played transposed

#### Scenario: Separated divergence episodes
- **WHEN** two sections diverge, share a sustained passage, and diverge again
- **THEN** the earlier continuation can end at sustained reconvergence
- **AND** the later episode has its own shared cue and contrast exercise
- **AND** one matching note between different continuations is not treated as sustained reconvergence

#### Scenario: Distant source sections and completion
- **WHEN** A and B occur far apart in the source
- **THEN** their exercise plays only the defined A/B excerpts with explicit re-entry context
- **AND** credit requires a successful complete A/B cycle under the current measured or manual practice rules, not success on only one alternative
- **AND** neither original-section nor adjacent-pair attainment is manufactured

### Requirement: Stable exercise identities and exclusions
Each exercise SHALL have a source-bound identity independent of earned progress and its exclusion state. Exclusion SHALL apply to exactly that identity and SHALL be reversible without discarding attainment. Excluded exercises SHALL be omitted from recommendations and aggregate assessment, while remaining inspectable and selectable.

#### Scenario: Surgical exclusion
- **WHEN** the player excludes one original occurrence, adjacent pair or contrast exercise
- **THEN** only that exercise is excluded
- **AND** equivalent occurrences, constituents and related exercises keep their own inclusion state
- **AND** exclusion does not mark prerequisites attained or grant any completion evidence

### Requirement: Evidence-preserving plan transition
Existing supported journals SHALL transition to the new exercise catalog without deleting raw measured/manual claims, historical joins, settings or source data. Only claims for exactly matching source-bound exercises SHALL contribute automatically to new attainment. Removed long joins and different section ranges SHALL remain recoverable without being counted as new exercises.

#### Scenario: Existing recursive plan
- **WHEN** a saved fixed-four or connection-boundary plan contains original attainment and a long joined claim
- **THEN** the current catalog uses the new bounded policy while retaining the original raw records
- **AND** exactly matching originals or pairs retain their legitimate evidence
- **AND** the long claim is not split into fabricated attainment for smaller exercises
- **AND** reopening is idempotent and a failed migration retains the last valid saved state

### Requirement: Player-controlled active exercise
The selected practice exercise SHALL remain active until the player explicitly chooses another exercise or leaves practice. Historical attainment, current success, target-speed completion, manual Done, newly unlocked connections and completion of the catalog SHALL NOT automatically select another exercise or open the selector. Normal repeats and speed progression SHALL continue within the selection.

#### Scenario: Slow replay of a completed section
- **WHEN** a section with historical target-speed achievement succeeds at 10% with a configured 2% increase
- **THEN** it repeats the same section at 12%, subject to existing count-in and repeat readiness rules
- **AND** the historical target-speed achievement remains intact
- **AND** a missing or late repeat receipt pauses that same section for explicit Resume

#### Scenario: Newly reach target speed
- **WHEN** the selected section first succeeds at target speed or repeats another success at that speed
- **THEN** the same exercise remains selected and normal repetition continues at the admitted rate
- **AND** a newly unlocked connection becomes available without being selected or started

#### Scenario: Complete the catalog or change manual evidence
- **WHEN** all catalog exercises reach their targets or an eligible Done/undo action changes completion
- **THEN** neither action selects another exercise or automatically returns to the selector
- **AND** speed locks, queued edits, reversible claims and save-failure handling retain their existing semantics

#### Scenario: Repeat a selected contrast
- **WHEN** the player completes A then B of a selected contrast exercise
- **THEN** A-to-B remains an internal transition and the next cycle returns to A of that same exercise
- **AND** credit requires the accepted complete cycle, with no automatic selection of another section or contrast

#### Scenario: Explicitly choose another exercise
- **WHEN** the player returns to the selector and activates another eligible exercise
- **THEN** that explicit selection changes the active exercise through its existing entry flow
- **AND** passive suggestions and browser highlighting alone cannot start or replace active practice
