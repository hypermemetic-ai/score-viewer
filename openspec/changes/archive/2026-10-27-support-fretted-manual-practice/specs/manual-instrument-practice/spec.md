# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Provide truthful manual musical practice for source-owned instrument notation independently of piano MIDI assessment and physical fingering.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Neutral manual capability
Complete admitted musical timing and notation ownership SHALL authorize manual practice independently of piano hands, keys, seats or fingering. A Bass/Guitar session SHALL remain manual even when a piano MIDI device is connected. Piano physical controls and assessment SHALL retain their existing genuine authority.

#### Scenario: Guitar practice with a connected piano
- **WHEN** a complete Guitar part is practised while piano MIDI is connected
- **THEN** manual practice and Done remain available
- **AND** piano notes do not match attacks, clear ink, fail the loop or create measured attainment

### Requirement: Shared musical progress
ISO/TAB practice of the same source part SHALL share passage, working-speed and reversible manual completion evidence. Distinct source parts SHALL keep independent evidence. Notation geometry/policy SHALL NOT define progress identity; existing piano journals and storage SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Complete and withdraw across notation
- **WHEN** the user marks an eligible passage Done in ISO and switches to TAB
- **THEN** the same manual result and tempo are visible and can be withdrawn under existing eligibility rules
- **AND** Include manual filtering retains evidence without treating it as measured data

### Requirement: Musical loop and explicit readable windows
Manual practice SHALL preserve source beat structure and the consistent count-in on every repeat. Previous/Next SHALL explicitly browse centered consecutive windows of up to four bars in two-bar steps during playback without stopping the clock or retiring note ink. Repeat count-in SHALL restore the initial window once.

#### Scenario: Five-bar leaf or long joined run
- **WHEN** the user turns a manual window during a five-bar leaf or multi-page joined run
- **THEN** complete source notation and cross-bar relations remain readable
- **AND** clock phase, tempo, completion eligibility, source obligations and evidence are unchanged
- **WHEN** the next repeat count-in actually starts
- **THEN** the initial window is restored without a continuously sliding display

### Requirement: Preserve manual and piano behavior
Manual notation SHALL retain full musical ink without fabricating observed attacks or automatic measured success. Musical options, speed lock/queue, first-loop eligibility, reversible Done, Free view, rotation and inherited navigation SHALL remain usable. Existing piano assessment and refill SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Rotation and loop completion
- **WHEN** the display rotates during manual playback or its clock reaches the loop end
- **THEN** full notation, musical phase and retained progress remain correct
- **AND** no measured success is recorded merely because time elapsed

### Requirement: Explicit source-part playing reference
Full notation reading and switching SHALL remain available without a performance reference. Entering manual Practice SHALL use the canonical source reference when present; otherwise it SHALL request an explicit playing reference and save it separately, bound to the exact source part and musical digest. Source tempo maps or words SHALL NOT be silently promoted into or rewritten in the canonical archive. The planner SHALL capture the fallback before its first await and bind the effective clock to the practice revision, while retaining the original source identity for view anchors. ISO/TAB SHALL share the same saved choice and manual journal; distinct parts SHALL NOT borrow it.

#### Scenario: Missing reference and cancelled choice
- **WHEN** a reference-less dual source enters Practice and the player cancels the chooser
- **THEN** the reader remains usable in both notations and all archive/library/reference bytes remain unchanged
- **WHEN** the player explicitly chooses a valid duration and rate
- **THEN** both notations can practice with the separate reference, including after a cold reopen
- **AND** a canonical source reference, including its retained unknown facts, remains authoritative and unchanged
