## Why

Actual practice loses readable staff frames, shrinks notation on MIDI Start, consumes speed-navigation keys without acting, and supplies an inadequate repeat cue. The user cannot begin a practice session until notation and count-ins are dependable. Current absolute-tempo defaults also produce a misleading 2.8% working speed, and identical repeated passages require duplicate completion.

## What Changes

- Keep a complete, stable four-measure surface across Ready, count-in and playing, with source-owned note retirement and full Free view restoration.
- Start fresh practice at 10% of the performance target and admit current speed controls in half-percentage-point units; queue live edits without altering an attempt's tempo.
- Supply two source-meter bars of count-in by default, adjustable in Options, using consistent metric hierarchy and exact source-entry phase on every repeat.
- Make the entire practice keyboard flow work predictably, including speed adjustment, Options browse/edit, Free view and return.
- Show an editable three-second error cooldown with a subtle centered lock and remaining seconds.
- Resolve genuinely identical passage and repeated-parent completion through shared musical equivalence without rewriting raw evidence.
- Deliver and inspect the reviewed repairs through the ordinary native lane, preserving personal data and prior packages.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `practice-passage-display`: stable mode geometry, current speed policy, musical repeat cues, keyboard flow, visible cooldown and exact musical completion equivalence.
- `practice-refill`: structural residency persists independently of retired note ink, including terminal allocation.

## Impact

Practice session/state, display/refill, shared Options and key routing, source pulse grids, native grid admission/scheduling, attainment interpretation and recipient verification. Existing score archives, historical evidence, piano/audio device policy and unrelated features remain intact. Square H/P source work proceeds separately and does not gate the urgent practice repair. A bounded repair delivery may retain the currently installed dependency rather than introduce unrelated unaccepted import or TAB changes.
