# Proposal

## Why

The user selected a clearer TAB vocabulary: fret numbers carry pitch, detached headless duration cues sit below the strings, black ink gives a consistent reading surface, and rests belong on the TAB staff. The user also approved simplifying only source-proven isolated rests whose written dots and tuplet ratio resolve exactly to an ordinary undotted rest. Adopting these display rules needs versioned source recipes and a safe additive presentation edition without reinterpreting historical archives or losing manual practice progress.

## What Changes

- Add a reviewed production presentation pair with upward TAB free tips at the close 14pt band and connecting rails/hook ends below; grouped labels stay below lower beams. Preserve existing ISO/TAB public defaults and literal replay.
- Bind black duration paint, short half/normal quarter/absent whole markers, source-owned hook/rail geometry, source-written dots/tuplets and staff-centered source rests to that recipe and complete geometry audits.
- Use 1.6pt augmentation squares with about 1.4pt edge/inter-square gaps close to genuine stem/rest bases; an unsimplified isolated source ratio stays visible below the staff. Better contextual treatment for a genuinely fractional orphan label remains unselected. Augmented whole-NOTE duration cues have no base stroke and their placement remains unsettled; the rest-only rule does not address them and no whole-symbol or fret-attached exception is selected.
- Share one source-owned decision between the new ISO and TAB recipes: only proven isolated rests with exact equivalent ordinary undotted values may use that derived display spelling. Explicit/complete/weighted/mixed or ambiguous groups and genuine fractions remain literal. Canonical written values, dots, ratios, source bytes and clocks never change.
- Dispatch the existing dual-reading descriptor through its per-view engine identity in actual Worker and independent receiving/cold paths, with no persisted admission certificate or DOM-based renderer substitute.
- Give intelligent continuity by merging only safe inferred beat groups overlapping an already-certified complete local tuplet. Keep a thin bracket/3 scoped to actual tuplet members, retain other ordinary groups and explicit syntax, and avoid a whole-bar beam default.
- Preserve canonical source bytes, owners, written/elapsed clocks, string/fret facts, authored beam facts/ISO topology and presentation-independent manual/reference journals. Historical ISO output remains literal; new ISO display differences require an explicit versioned recipe and the exact isolated-rest proof, not a reinterpretation of the old ISO engine.
- Add an explicit admitted same-source retained-style edition install/selection path that preserves old archives, library identity and personal pointers; keep generic conflicting imports refused.
- Keep product controls compact: fresh imports select the accepted renderer internally, and retained editions use existing applicable flows without a new TAB style picker, renderer-version labels or rhythm commentary in Options.
- Validate normal-scale full-page and joined notation, keyboard/manual behavior, cancellation, preservation and later ordinary paired native delivery separately.
- Consume the resolved source-written audit from merged ISO PR352 `cbdd38f2b920c09765e3a8517cc29ab45330f87a` only through a deliberate reviewed consumer pin, with half/dotted-triplet and exact historical receipt checks; retain unchanged painter/clocks and malformed-source refusal.
