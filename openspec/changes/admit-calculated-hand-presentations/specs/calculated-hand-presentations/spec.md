# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Admit calculator-selected performing hands as complete compatible score editions while preserving immutable musical source and independently guarded personal history.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Joint performing-hand preparation
New calculations SHALL choose performing hands jointly with fingers and physical contacts. Printed staves, inferred hands and derived calculator hands SHALL NOT become blanket source or operator constraints. Genuine explicit source and guarded owner choices SHALL remain enforced.

#### Scenario: Accompaniment enters the melody hand
- **WHEN** a complete unconstrained proposal transfers the highest accompaniment note to the melody hand
- **THEN** preparation evaluates that route for its actual keyboard and requests a complete compatible engraving without changing independent source voices

#### Scenario: Retain imported hands explicitly
- **WHEN** the operator explicitly requests calculation within the imported hand division
- **THEN** that request records its distinct hand policy and retains that division without relabeling it as composer authority

### Requirement: Conservative authority admission
New requests SHALL exclude only positively identified automatic editorial-retention constraints. Valid source/operator guards SHALL preserve the declared hand, finger and contact for their affected owners. Unknown, incomplete, contradictory or stale authority SHALL produce an explicit conflict and retain the current edition.

#### Scenario: Known automatic retention
- **WHEN** a saved assignment contains the Viewer-generated hand-only automatic retention record with a matching source guard
- **THEN** a new joint-hand request removes that record while retaining genuine guarded operator constraints

#### Scenario: Ambiguous retention label
- **WHEN** a record claims automatic retention but has unknown provenance, extra choices or a stale source guard
- **THEN** preparation reports an authority conflict rather than silently discarding it

#### Scenario: Unrelated personal history
- **WHEN** one owner has a current correction and another owner has only historical choices
- **THEN** only the current owner's declared guarded choices constrain new calculation; unrelated hands remain searchable and historical choices stay with their edition

### Requirement: Complete calculation and presentation identity
Reusable readiness SHALL match immutable source, canonical complete assignment, keyboard/profile, calculation context, hand policy, guarded constraints and rendering recipe/payload. Assignment ordering SHALL NOT change identity. A legacy saved layer SHALL NOT bypass a requested new policy or required preparation.

#### Scenario: Policy changes beneath a saved layer
- **WHEN** the installed layer retained all published hands and a new request permits joint-hand search
- **THEN** the old layer cannot satisfy the new calculation solely because its planner version is current

#### Scenario: Context or recipe differs
- **WHEN** the playing tempo, keyboard profile, guarded choice or rendering recipe changes
- **THEN** cached readiness is not reused without matching compatibility evidence

#### Scenario: Equivalent reordered assignments
- **WHEN** identical complete owner choices arrive in a different array order
- **THEN** they have the same derived identity and different choices have a different identity

### Requirement: Fresh complete engraving admission
Changed performing hands SHALL use complete freshly prepared ISO pages, seats and source-owned audit evidence under a compatible reviewed recipe. Admission SHALL independently reimport the assembled package and reject missing coverage, stale geometry, source changes or unsupported required grace/fit. Frozen pages SHALL NOT be relabeled or arbitrarily reflowed.

#### Scenario: Independent voice rest on a busy hand
- **WHEN** accompaniment transfers to a hand whose independent melody voice rests
- **THEN** admitted pages retain the melody silence with its source affiliation and independent source-statement coverage

#### Scenario: Mixed-hand beam and written tie continuation
- **WHEN** a transferred attack belongs to an authored beam with a written continuation
- **THEN** admission requires the complete source gesture, tie and hand-specific owned geometry supplied by ISO

#### Scenario: Incompatible recipe or unsupported fit
- **WHEN** retained recipe reconstruction fails or the proposed presentation has unsupported fit or required grace-hand coverage
- **THEN** preparation returns a specific refusal and the exact prior package remains active

### Requirement: Guarded personal edition compatibility
Corrections SHALL carry into a new edition only after explicit source-owner, hand, action/contact, profile and seat compatibility checks. Earlier calculated layers, undo journals, pages and practice history SHALL remain associated with their compatible editions. Edits SHALL NOT be silently attached to a different hand revision.

#### Scenario: One corrected owner and other transferred owners
- **WHEN** the corrected owner retains its guarded physical choice and other owners transfer hands
- **THEN** a compatible correction is preserved and prior edition journals remain retained; old-hand layers are not offered on the new-hand pages

#### Scenario: Changed alias or correction contact
- **WHEN** a new presentation changes an edited owner's action/contact affiliation or compatible physical profile
- **THEN** admission reports the compatibility conflict and keeps the prior edition and correction journal

#### Scenario: Earlier edition selected
- **WHEN** the operator selects a retained earlier edition
- **THEN** its own frozen pages, seats and compatible saved history are loaded together without automatic regeneration

### Requirement: Atomic retained installation
New package, seats, layer and compatible personal state SHALL be fully staged and checked before one library pointer activates them. Failure, cancellation or supersession SHALL retain the previous referenced files and edition. Original source bytes and prior packages SHALL remain recoverable.

#### Scenario: Staging fails before activation
- **WHEN** package/state writing or the final active-job check fails
- **THEN** the library continues to reference the old complete package and personal state

#### Scenario: Both keyboard alternatives
- **WHEN** a conventional and four-row Janko edition are prepared in either order
- **THEN** each has its own matching pages/seats/layer and the other keyboard's existing presentation retains its exact bytes

### Requirement: Honest regression and acceptance evidence
Validation SHALL use independently authored public fixtures and source-derived expectations, with adversarial deletion, duplication and attribution controls. Complete real-engine pages and focused passages SHALL be reviewed before changed-hand activation. Static assignment and estimated transport SHALL remain distinct from continuous-motion certification.

#### Scenario: Transformation edge cases
- **WHEN** testing selective transfers in both directions, independent same-hand voices, unequal-release unisons, repeats, written continuations and page/system boundaries
- **THEN** source fidelity, actual painted ownership and edition/history compatibility are checked independently of the renderer's grouping

#### Scenario: Native or musical acceptance unavailable
- **WHEN** Linux checks pass without a coordinated native installation or player review
- **THEN** evidence identifies that limit and does not claim installed-app or musical acceptance

### Requirement: Staged coordinated dependency adoption
Changed-hand activation SHALL require an exact reviewed ISO dependency and its compatible recipe/audit contract. Existing application identity, user data, devices and active delivery SHALL be preserved. Historical delivery limitations SHALL remain historical records.

#### Scenario: ISO plan exists but implementation is pending
- **WHEN** only planning artifacts or an unreviewed engine candidate are available
- **THEN** Viewer can develop independent gates but cannot pin or activate changed-hand rendering from a speculative engine identity

#### Scenario: Concurrent native delivery
- **WHEN** another owner is delivering the native app
- **THEN** this change preserves that delivery and waits for an actual coordinated activation boundary
